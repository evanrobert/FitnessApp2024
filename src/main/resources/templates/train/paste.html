Tips

What we understand

Put each exercise on its own line, then its sets underneath. Bullets, dashes and numbers are fine.

  • 50 lbs 10 reps one set
  • 135 x 8 135 lb, 8 times
  • 3x10 @ 135 3 sets of 10 at 135 lb
  • 3 sets of 12 at 50 lbs
  • 185 lbs: 8, 8, 6 three sets
  • Push-ups 3x15 exercise and sets on one line
  • 12 reps or bodyweight x 12
  • 60 kg x 5 converted to pounds

A first line like "Push day" becomes the workout's name, and a date like 10/3 sets the day. Nothing is saved until you press Save.
